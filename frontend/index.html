❓ Help Center & FAQs

Frequently asked questions regarding VME mechanics, electricians, and rules

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does VME guarantee verified mechanics and electricians?
A: All technicians undergo physical garage audits, trade license checks, and background verification before getting onboarded.
Q: Can I call and chat with the technician directly inside this app?
A: Yes! VME features native built-in VoIP calling and real-time chat with live location sharing. No external dialer or apps needed.
Q: How does the technician find my exact breakdown spot?
A: Your GPS pin is displayed live on the technician's in-app radar screen with turn-by-turn route calculation.
